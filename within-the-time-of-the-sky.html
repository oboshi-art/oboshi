About this painting

Light before it becomes light. The white ground carries colour the way atmosphere carries weather — loosely, without insisting. Yellow ignites the upper left; orange forms pulse at the centre like something still deciding whether to stay. The right half breathes out, quieter, marks dissolving into the pale field. There is no drama here, only duration. The painting exists inside a moment that is neither arriving nor departing — simply suspended, vibrating at its own unhurried frequency.
