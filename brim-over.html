About this painting

The painting is at the edge of its own capacity — and it knows it. Red and orange mass on the left with a heat that feels almost muscular; blue opens on the right like a window left ajar. Between them, mark-making spills into something close to language without becoming it. Black script-like forms rise through the centre, illegible but urgent. The grey field does not contain the energy. It simply documents where the overflow has already been.
