About this painting

The painting does not escape — it accumulates. Layer upon layer, colour upon colour, each fragment pressing against the next with nowhere left to go. Yellow surges through the centre like a current that cannot decide its direction; black anchors the lower register, heavy with what has been left behind. And yet the overall feeling is not of entrapment but of discovery — the kind that only happens when you stop looking for the exit and begin to read the walls instead. The dream was already here, buried in the layers.
