About this painting

White dominates — not as absence but as the texture of not-yet. Thick, worked, impasto-heavy, it holds the composition in a state of suspension while colour waits at the edges: red urgent at the upper left, teal and yellow considered at the upper right, black and gold grounding the lower corners. Small dark forms emerge through the white like thoughts that have almost surfaced. The decision has not been made. But the surface shows every layer of preparation — every revision, every near-commitment that white has covered over. It is all still there, just beneath.
