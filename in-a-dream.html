About this painting

Yellow runs through the centre like a current remembered from somewhere else — warm, unhurried, certain of its direction even as everything around it fragments. Red forms cluster on the left, urgent and slightly unresolved; blue and black structures rise on the right with the logic of a city seen from above in sleep. The small outlined rectangles scattered throughout feel like windows, or memories of windows. The painting does not announce itself as a dream. It simply operates by dream logic: presence without explanation, connection without cause, colour that arrives before the image does.
