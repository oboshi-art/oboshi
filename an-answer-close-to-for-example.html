About this painting

Patterns collide and dissolve — grids, stripes, dots, radiating lines, each speaking a different dialect of mark-making. Nothing is resolved, but nothing is abandoned either. The white ground moves through the layers like breath, separating what needs space and holding what needs to remain. A golden circle anchors the upper right; a deep violet form grounds the lower left. Between them, the painting searches — not for a conclusion, but for the closest possible approximation of one. This is what it feels like to reach toward an answer that keeps shifting its shape.
