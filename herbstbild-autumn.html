About this painting

I painted it as if someone were singing a song that gives the feeling of something filling up and then returning again. Crimson and burnt orange gather across the upper field like a held note, dripping downward into forest green. Yellow-green torn-tape stripes fall through the lower half like the song's own rhythm, repeating and repeating. Pale mint and white vinyl panels sit to the right — a quieter register, where the singing softens but never quite stops.

Part of "Shiki (四季)", OBOSHI's four-painting contribution to the group exhibition "Meguru (廻る)" — Schöneberger Art 2026, Q Gallery Berlin, November 7–8.
