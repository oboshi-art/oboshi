About this painting

Nothing here moves in one direction. Stripes run vertically, horizontally, diagonally — each fragment insisting on its own rhythm while pressed against fragments insisting on theirs. The density is total: pattern upon pattern, collage upon collage, every surface already occupied when the next layer arrived. And yet the painting does not feel blocked. It feels held — as if the tangles are not problems to be solved but currents to be carried, each one part of a larger circulation that only becomes visible when you stop trying to follow any single thread.
