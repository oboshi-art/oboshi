About this painting

The vast green is not emptiness — it is the feeling of having room to think. Collage fragments drift at the edges and centre like thoughts that surface briefly before dissolving back into the field. Walking in the mind has no destination. It moves by association, by colour, by the quiet pull of one thing toward another. This painting holds that particular lightness — the freedom of going nowhere in particular, and finding something there.
