Art for spaces

Paintings for the rooms people spend time in

Original abstract paintings and commissions for clinics and practices, offices, hotels and restaurants, and staged homes — made by hand in Berlin.

OBOSHI paints in layers of acrylic, hand-painted torn washi tape and transparent film. The surfaces are dense in some places and open in others, so the work stays interesting up close and calm from across the room. It does not tell a fixed story; each person who passes by can read it in their own way.

How a commission works

  • Brief. Tell me about the room: size, light, wall colour, who uses it, and the mood you want.
  • Proposal. I suggest sizes and a colour direction, with a price.
  • Painting. About three weeks in the Berlin studio.
  • Delivery. Packed and insured, shipped worldwide via DHL (shipping is charged separately), with a certificate of authenticity.
