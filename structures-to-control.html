About this painting

The painting knows what it is doing and does it anyway. Collage fragments, painted marks, and drawn elements press against each other in a density that suggests not chaos but system — many systems, overlapping, each one insisting on its own logic. Blue and red hold the upper register with authority; green forms anchor the lower left like roots of something that has decided to stay. The structure is not imposed from outside. It emerges from the accumulation itself — the way order appears when enough layers have been laid down and the relationships between them begin to stabilise.
