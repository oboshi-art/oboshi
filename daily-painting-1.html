About this painting

Blue is not a colour here — it is a world. Dense, immersive, total. Layers of cobalt and ultramarine build a field so complete that the collage fragments beneath become archaeology — glimpsed through the surface like objects seen through deep water. At the lower centre, a red form breaks through with sudden heat, refusing to be subsumed. Yellow edges the periphery. The painting asks the viewer to stop moving, to let the blue frequency settle into the body before looking for anything else.
