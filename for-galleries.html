Short biography

Berlin-based abstract painter. From 2002 to 2007, he was the first Japanese artist to become a resident of the legendary art squat Kunsthaus Tacheles. He has continued to live and work in Berlin for over two decades since. Working with acrylic paint, hand-painted and hand-torn washi tape, and layers of clear film, he builds paintings through a distinctive layering technique that holds both density and stillness. His work is not intended to convey a fixed story or meaning, but to leave the experience open to each viewer’s own perception.
