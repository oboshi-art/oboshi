About this painting

Verbindung (“Connection”) is one large painting made of 26 smaller ones. Joined, they become a single image; apart, each one still stands entirely on its own.

Each of the 26 paintings carries its own meaning and its own wish, and each is meant for one place — one of the 25 countries of the European Union at the time, and Japan. Which panel travels to which country is left open; only Japan was fixed from the start. When all of them are brought together, the wishes become one.

That one is the 50 × 40 cm panel near the centre of the whole. It is the actual palette on which the other 25 paintings were made — so the colours of all 25 European paintings meet on Japan, and are literally joined.

To share one piece of the large painting can also mean to share one wish: connection, coexistence, contentment, and understanding each other. The work is made to connect the 25 countries of the EU and Japan — and, through them, Japanese and European culture.
