About this painting

The painting is already inside the store. Red and pink saturate the field the way commercial space saturates attention — fully, without asking. Price tags surface from the collage layer: €0.69, €1.19, €1.29, €1.69 — small, specific, almost tender in their ordinariness. The word "shopping" appears in blue at the bottom, handwritten, as if someone needed to name what was already happening. Japanese text drifts through the centre alongside German fragments. This is not critique. It is participation — the genuine, slightly overwhelming pleasure of wanting things, written in every frequency at once.
