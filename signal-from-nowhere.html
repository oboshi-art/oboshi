About this painting

Layers of collaged materials — prints of past works, washi paper, and hand-painted tape — form the foundation of this painting, built up further with acrylic in a process of accumulation and erasure. Vertical white strokes cut through the composition, creating space to breathe between color and form. Magenta, blue, and teal resonate across the canvas, generating a quiet, persistent vibration.

The work leaves the viewer with a sensation of something received from a distant, unidentifiable place.

The signal does not announce itself. White vertical strokes move through the painting like interference patterns — present, rhythmic, neither noise nor message but something between. Blue holds the upper register with depth; teal, pink, green, and ochre layer beneath and between the white, visible in the gaps where the signal has not yet passed. Small rectangular forms appear at intervals, like windows briefly open. This painting does not ask where the signal comes from. It records the fact of its arrival — the frequency that reaches the body before the mind can name its source.
