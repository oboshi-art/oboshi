About this painting

The painting divides itself — not by intention but by gravity. The upper left breathes in white, holding scattered forms like thoughts that have not yet found their weight: pink circles, soft polygons, things still becoming. The right half descends into depth — teal, black, blue building in planes that press against each other with increasing density. Between the two, a diagonal current pulls everything slowly toward the darker register. Thought does not stay light forever. The painting knows this, and does not resist it.
