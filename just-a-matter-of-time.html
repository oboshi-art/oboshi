About this painting

The painting waits. A wide pale field holds its marks loosely — script-like forms at the top that almost speak, a blue circle on the left that has been here before, colour blocks scattered through the lower register like appointments not yet kept. Nothing is urgent. Nothing is finished. The orange square at the bottom edge pulses quietly against the white, and the green square to the right simply exists, certain and patient. Time is not running out here. It is accumulating — layer by layer, mark by mark, in no particular hurry.
